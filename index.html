Why solar power is now big business

Read, listen, learn the vocabulary, and test yourself. About 15 minutes.

Read

Topic: renewable energy · 270 words

Twenty years ago, solar panels were expensive and mostly used for small projects, such as powering a calculator or a house in a remote area. Today, they are one of the cheapest ways to produce electricity in many countries. The cost of solar panels has fallen sharply, and this change has attracted investors, governments and large companies.

Most solar projects follow a similar model. A developer finds land, gets the necessary permits, and builds a solar farm. Then it signs a power purchase agreement, or PPA, with a buyer. In a PPA, the buyer agrees to purchase the electricity at a fixed price for many years, often between 10 and 25 years. This gives the developer a stable income and makes it easier to get a bank loan.

However, solar energy has a challenge: the sun does not shine at night. Because of this, the electricity grid must balance supply and demand at every moment. Batteries can store extra energy during the day and release it in the evening, when demand is high. For this reason, many new projects combine solar panels with storage.

For businesses, renewable energy is not only about the environment. Many companies install solar panels to reduce their electricity bills and protect themselves from price increases. Others buy renewable energy to meet their sustainability goals. As a result, professionals who understand both energy and finance are increasingly in demand.
